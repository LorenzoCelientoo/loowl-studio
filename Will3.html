ISSUE NO. 01 / BACKGROUND

Why join Highlands
Endurance Club?

In 2023, after a long season of isolation and a lack of motivation, I committed to running. I decided to make running a habit. That habit led to progress, progress fueled motivation, and away we went. But something was missing.

I wanted to take my running to the next level, but I knew I couldn't do it alone. I found a running coach and enjoyed a rich (virtual) running community that spurred me on and equipped me in ways I didn't know I needed.

After seeing dramatic improvement under my coach, my own personal circumstances led to ending my time receiving coaching and writing my own plans. Through this, and working for my friend's running coaching business, I've come to love the art and science of running coaching.

Whether you're new to running or wanting to take your running to the next level, Highlands Endurance Club is for you. Through coaching you will experience intentionality, continuity, and community that will guide you onward. Together, let's hit the heights.

— Coach Will

What you can expect:

  • 01 / Custom Training Plan

    A monthly custom running plan, complete with daily runs, workouts, and long runs tailored to your half or full marathon goals.

    An in-depth process detailing your current running background, lifestyle, past injuries, and future race goals.

  • 03 / Community Focus

    A true team feel sustained through a dedicated group message and an exclusive Strava group.

  • 04 / Peak Race Strategy

    A comprehensive race plan curated specifically for the week of your peak race target.

  • 05 / Post-Race Review

    A detailed review after your peak race to analyze performance and plan your next building block.

  • 06 / Direct Coach Access

    Ongoing access to me to answer any and all questions in relation to your training throughout the entire block.
